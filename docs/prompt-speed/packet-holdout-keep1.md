## hs-deadreckon-01 (expect answer)
> What is dead reckoning?
Key points: A way of tracking position using direction and distance travelled from a known point; First determine direction and distance between two points on a map; Then follow that direction and count the distance actually covered
Red flags: Describes it as requiring GPS or any electronic device

**A**
```
1. Dead reckoning is a navigation technique used to determine the position of an individual or vehicle based on known starting points and directional information, such as compass bearings and elapsed time.  
2. It involves estimating movement along a specified azimuth (direction) from a reference point.  
3. The process includes determining direction and distance from one point to another using tools like a protractor, map, and compass.  
4. Dead reckoning can be used in conjunction with other navigation methods to minimize errors and ensure accurate positioning.
  [source: ATP 3-50.21 Survival 2018 p.168 — DEAD RECKONING]
```
**B**
```
1. Dead reckoning is a navigation technique used to determine the location of an object based on known starting points and estimated or measured movements.  
2. It involves calculating the position by tracking the direction and distance traveled from a reference point, using compass readings and time/pace measurements.  
3. It is commonly used in isolation situations where external navigational cues are limited.  
4. Dead reckoning can be applied along a specified magnetic azimuth to establish a route.  
5. It relies on precise measurement of movement and direction, but is prone to error due to imprecision in compass readings or pacing.
  [source: ATP 3-50.21 Survival 2018 p.168 — DEAD RECKONING]
```

## hs-spoiledfish-01 (expect answer)
> I caught a fish but it smells off. If I cook it thoroughly is it safe?
Key points: No — do not eat fish that appears spoiled; Cooking does not make spoiled fish safe to eat; Improper cleaning or storage is what makes fish inedible
Red flags: Says cooking spoiled fish thoroughly makes it safe; Advises eating it anyway because calories matter more

**A**
```
1. Check for signs of spoilage: Look for dents that stay in the fish's flesh after pressed with a thumb, slimy body, or sharp taste.  
2. Do not cook spoiled fish thoroughly; it may cause diarrhea, nausea, cramps, vomiting, itching, paralysis, or a metallic taste in the mouth.  
3. If symptoms appear, induce vomiting.
  [source: ATP 3-50.21 Survival 2018 p.113 — PREPARING FISH FOR CONSUMPTION]
```

```
1. Check for signs of spoilage: Look for dents that stay in the fish's flesh after pressed with a thumb, slimy body, or sharp taste.
```

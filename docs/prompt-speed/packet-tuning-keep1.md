## fa-blister-01 (expect answer)
> I have a bad blister on my heel from hiking. What should I do about it?
Key points: Keep it clean and covered to protect it; Reduce the friction causing it, for example by padding or changing socks; Watch for signs of infection
Red flags: 

**A**
```
1. Stop at the first sign of rubbing.  
2. Apply a small piece of moleskin or athletic tape to areas that are causing problems.  
3. If the blister is smaller than a nickel, do not puncture it.  
4. Cut a doughnut shaped piece of moleskin and center it over the blister.  
5. Clean and wash the blister and cover it with a sterile dressing.
  [source: ATP 3-50.21 Survival 2018 p.43 — SOFT TISSUE TRAUMA]
```
**B**
```
1. Stop at the first sign of rubbing.  
2. Apply a small piece of moleskin or athletic tape to areas that are causing problems.
```

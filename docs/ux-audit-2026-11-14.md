# UX/UI audit, 2026-11-14

First run of the `ux-ui-review` skill (`.claude/skills/ux-ui-review/`), on
the Android app at `84a6e81`. Test branch `ux-cleanup-test`; the test APK
installs beside the real app as **Fred UX test** (`dev.fred.app.uxtest`).

Ryan asked for everything in scope, including the side menu (#211) and
how sources are shown (#215). Both are proposals to judge on the phone,
not decisions.

## What was looked at

Empty chat, a written answer, a card answer, a folded card offer, a
decline, an error, a stopped answer, needs-location, clarify buttons, the
side menu and past chats, About, the loading and failed-load screens.
Not changed: the map screen, setup, and the day/night sky.

## Findings

Severity: **S1** could cost someone in an emergency, **S2** slows or
confuses, **S3** polish.

| # | Where | Rule | Found | Sev | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Error reply | States: plain words and a way on | The raw exception text was the whole reply; no next step | S1 | Plain sentence, what to try, an **Ask again** button; the message kept as small "Details" |
| 2 | "Don't" block | Contrast: 7:1 for safety text | Bold warning words 4.7:1 on blush by day, 5.7:1 by night | S1 | New `warn` colour: 7.3:1 and 7.5:1 |
| 3 | Medical disclaimer | Contrast and size | 6.6:1 by day at 12 sp | S2 | 7.9:1, 13 sp |
| 4 | Secondary text (sources, hints, notes) | Contrast in sunlight; 13 sp floor | moss 5.2:1 by day; 11-13 sp in many places | S2 | moss 6.2:1; hints 14, notes 13, headings 14-15 in sentence case |
| 5 | State, clarify and location buttons | Touch targets 48 dp | About 38-40 dp tall | S2 | `minHeight: 48` (and `minWidth` for the two-letter states) |
| 6 | "What to do" fold, "Open the map" | Touch targets | A text line with an 8 pt hitSlop, about 36 dp | S2 | Full-width 48 dp rows |
| 7 | Ask button, text box | Touch targets | 44 dp | S3 | 48 dp |
| 8 | Sources after every answer (#215) | AI trust: basis visible, passage reachable, no heavy repeat | A full block every reply; book and section heading only, no passage | S2 | One line, "Sources · ATP 4-02.11 First Aid 2026", that opens to each passage (long ones folded), the page and section; Wikivoyage credit and link kept with its passage |

Contrast after the changes (`contrast.py`): every safety pair is at 7:1 or
more in both palettes; every other text pair at 4.5:1 or more.

## Not changed, on purpose

- **Words of answers and cards.** Measured separately; a UI pass doesn't
  touch them.
- **The greetings.** They are calm and are Ryan's tone. The starters
  under them now do the work of getting someone going.
- **Medical disclaimer placement.** It already sits right under the
  answer, where NN/g says it should be.
- **The menu tab itself.** It sits opposite the logo and already has a
  generous target (30 x 52 plus a 12 pt margin).
- **Map and setup screens.** Not reviewed this round.

## To check on the phone

- In bright sun: the sources line, hints and the disclaimer.
- Remove a past chat, then Undo; let one time out.

## Third pass: the setup wizard

| # | Where | Rule | Found | Sev | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22 | Day and night page | Cognitive load: fewer things between a person and their goal | A whole page explaining the colour switch; the last page's tour already points at the sun | S2 | Page removed: four steps, not five |
| 23 | Guides page copy | Plain, short | "the part of me that reads them and writes your answer" | S3 | "Everything I need to answer you, kept on this phone." |
| 24 | Downloading | Stress: don't make people wait on one thing at a time when they needn't | Next was disabled for the whole 1.3 GB download | S2 | A download queue that keeps going between pages: pick states and read the tour meanwhile. Only Start waits |
| 25 | Progress | States: loading says how long | Bytes only ("640 MB of 1.3 GB") | S2 | "· about 4 min left", from the speed so far, after 4 s, in whole minutes |
| 26 | Storage | States: check before the tap, not after | Not enough room was reported only as a failure | S2 | "This phone has 42 GB free" beside the size; the button is off, with a reason, when it won't fit |
| 27 | Cancel | Undo over loss; targets | A small text link; reads as losing everything | S2 | **Pause** and **Resume** (48 dp); what arrived is kept, and the strip says so |
| 28 | Failure | States: plain words and a way on | The raw error under the card | S2 | "The download of Oregon stopped. The connection may have dropped. What arrived is kept." and **Try again** |
| 29 | Several downloads | Feedback | Each state's own bar in the list; nothing overall | S3 | One strip on every page: "Downloading Oregon · 2 of 3", bar, time left, Pause. The list says "Downloading now" or "Waiting its turn" |
| 30 | Back, Skip | Touch targets | About 40 dp | S3 | 48 dp |

## Fourth pass (2026-11-15): the map and offline maps

The two screens the first three passes left out, reviewed with the skill,
plus research on permission requests (Braze, Radar and OneSignal on
location priming) and NN/g's *AI Chatbots: Design Guidelines*. Iteration 3
is tagged `ux-iteration-3`, and its APK and screenshots are saved in
`~/fred-runs/ux-iterations/3/`.

| # | Where | Rule | Found | Sev | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | Opening the map | Permissions: say why before the system asks; ask on a goal-directed tap | Android's location dialog came up the moment the map opened, with no word from Fred first | S2 | With access already given, unchanged. Otherwise the sheet says what location is for and that it stays on the phone, and **Find where I am** brings the system's question |
| 32 | Location blocked | Never a dead end | "Turn it on in Settings", with no way there | S2 | **Open Settings** button |
| 33 | Map sheet text | 13 sp floor; no small capitals | Legend, accuracy pill, picker hint 12 sp; "CARRIER" / "OFFLINE MAPS" 11 sp capitals | S2 | 13 sp, sentence case |

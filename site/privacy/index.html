Fred · Privacy policy

Your questions stay on your phone.

Fred is a field guide that runs entirely on your phone: first aid, survival, and the plants and animals around you. He doesn't need an account, and he answers without signal. This page says what stays on your phone and the few things that leave it.

Effective 19 November 2026 · Fred is in beta

In short

  • No account. Fred never asks who you are.
  • Your location never leaves your phone.
  • Your chats stay on your phone, unless you choose to rate a reply.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What stays on your phone

These are kept in Fred's own storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.

  • Your questions and Fred's answers. Fred keeps your recent chats so you can read them again. You can remove any chat from the menu, and deleting the app deletes them all.
  • Your location. When you allow it, Fred uses your location to pick the right local plant and animal guide, show you on the map, plan routes, and point you toward cell coverage. All of this is worked out on the phone. Fred has no server that receives your location.
  • Your settings and setup: day or night colours, which maps you downloaded, and when you agreed to the beta notice.

What leaves your phone, and why

1. Ratings you choose to send

Under each reply there are two small thumbs. Nothing is sent unless you tap one. When you do, Fred sends this to the Fred team:

  • thumbs up or thumbs down, and anything you type in "What can Fred do better?";
  • the question you asked and Fred's reply, with the guides it cited;
  • whether you stopped the answer early, and which answering mode Fred used;
  • when you asked and when you rated;
  • Fred's version number and your phone's operating system and version (for example, "iOS 18.2").

A rating does not include your location, your name, your contacts, or any ID for you or your phone, and nothing meant to tie one rating to another or to you. If you're out of signal, the rating waits on your phone and is sent the next time Fred can reach the internet. Once it has been sent, it is removed from your phone.

Please don't type personal details (names, phone numbers, addresses, health details about a particular person) into a question you plan to rate, or into the comment box.

Ratings are stored in a database run on Cloudflare, in the United States. Only the Fred team can read them. They are used for one thing: finding where Fred's answers are wrong, unclear or missing something, and fixing them. They are never shown to other people who use Fred, and never sold or shared.

2. Downloads

Setting Fred up downloads his guides and the maps you pick from GitHub, where they are published. Like any website, GitHub sees your phone's internet address when it sends a file. Fred sends nothing else with the request. See GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Your internet address

Any time a phone connects to a server, the server sees its internet (IP) address. Cloudflare uses it for a moment to stop anyone flooding the ratings server. It is not saved with your rating, and the Fred team doesn't keep it.

4. Links you open

Some sources have a credit link. Tapping one opens that website in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

5. TestFlight (iPhone beta testers)

If you test Fred through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with us under Apple's privacy policy: crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight itself. Fred does not add anything to what TestFlight collects.

How long ratings are kept

Ratings are kept while Fred is being developed and tested, and deleted when they are no longer useful for improving him.

Your choices

  • Don't rate. Fred works exactly the same if you never tap a thumb.
  • Turn off location. You can refuse or withdraw location in your phone's settings. Fred will ask which state you're in when it needs to know.
  • Delete your chats from Fred's menu, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone, including any ratings still waiting to be sent.
  • Ask for a rating to be deleted. Because ratings carry no ID, we find yours by what it said. Get in touch with roughly when you rated and what the question was about. A short description is enough; please don't paste the full text.

Children

Fred is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly receive ratings from them. If you think a child has sent one, tell us and we will delete it.

Fred's advice

Fred is in beta and can be wrong. His answers are general information from published guides, not medical or professional advice. Trust your own judgement, and in an emergency, contact emergency services first if you can.

Changes to this policy

If what Fred collects changes, this page will change first, with a new date at the top. Every past version is kept in the project's history.
